AI in production at ShareMatch

Three AI features I worked on at ShareMatch, the world's first agentic AI market for Shariah-compliant sports investing. The code is private to the company.

HAL-AI

ShareMatch's proprietary AI engine for real-time market analysis, sports intelligence and Shariah-compliance screening. Users chat with it by text or voice, in English and Arabic. It answers from live Google Search results with cited sources, reviews the user's own holdings, quotes real prices from our database rather than the web, and can pre-fill a trade from plain language. Answers stream over SSE, and betting sources are filtered out for Shariah compliance.

FAQ Chatbot

The platform's RAG based help assistant. A fast LLM call first decides whether the user wants a how-to video or an answer. Answers come from retrieval over the FAQ library, with a strict answer-only-from-sources prompt and a filter that keeps logged-in content away from visitors.
